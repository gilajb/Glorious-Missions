## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a profound sense of hope, warmth, cultural grounding, and humanitarian purpose. Created for a Christian missions organization rooted in authentic visual storytelling, the design style blends modern editorial clarity with the tactile soul of East African cultural symbolism—specifically drawing from the iconic Maasai Shuka cloth traditions.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on:
- **Warm Editorial Dignity:** Treating every mission narrative, photograph, and portrait with reverence. High contrast, generous negative space, and curated typography elevate storytelling above standard institutional tropes.
- **Symbolic Vibrancy:** Color is not mere decoration; it speaks directly to life, faith, and communal fellowship. Tones resonate with earth, sky, unity, and open hospitality.
- **Textile & Geometric Subtlety:** Micro-details—such as finely drawn crossbars, border insets, subtle rhythm bars, and delicate grid accents—quietly echo the geometry of woven Shuka wraps without overwhelming content readability.
- **Humanist Tactility:** Surfaces feel natural, grounded, and welcoming, using warm off-whites, terracotta-infused shadows, and rounded, friendly structural bounds that invite trust and generous engagement.

## Colors

The palette is directly anchored in Maasai Shuka color symbolism, mapped intentionally to functional product hierarchies:

- **Primary Shuka Red (`#C92A2A`):** Symbolizes bravery, sacrificial love, strength, and community solidarity. Dedicated to primary calls-to-action (Donate, Go, Support), interactive highlights, active badges, and essential mission impact metrics.
- **Sustenance Blue (`#1E429F`):** Represents the open sky, clean water, and divine providence. Used for reflective hero banners, devotional insights, quiet theological foundations, and primary header backdrops.
- **Land & Community Green (`#1E7E34`):** Reflects the fertile earth, harvest, shelter, and community health. Applied to outreach initiatives, clinic reports, sustainable farming dispatches, and positive progress meters.
- **Hospitality Ochre & Sun Orange (`#E08722`):** Embodies welcome, warming hearths, and generous fellowship. Serves as an accent tone for "Get Involved," volunteer cards, and fellowship invitations.
- **Grounding Black/Charcoal (`#1A1715`):** A warm, deep charcoal-black that provides editorial contrast for body copy, navigational headers, and authoritative footers, avoiding cold digital pure black.
- **Pure Canvas & Off-White (`#FCFBF7` and `#FFFFFF`):** Representing purity and peaceful transparency. Serves as the breathable foundation for photography displays, narrative reading, and uncluttered layouts.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial pairing between classical human dignity and clear modern utility:

- **Headline Family (Noto Serif):** Brings warmth, authority, and classical elegance. It conveys historical weight and timeless integrity, ideal for missionary testimonials, theological vision, and featured photography titles.
- **Body & Label Family (Work Sans):** A humanist, clear sans-serif with open apertures and solid proportions. Ensures effortless legibility across dense field dispatches, donation steps, and multilingual community newsletters.
- **Case and Tracking:** Display and headline levels leverage slight negative letter-spacing for tight editorial impact. Labels, section categories, and metadata tags utilize uppercase tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) to echo structured, clean field reports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an organic 12-column fluid grid system bounded at a maximum container width of `1320px`, allowing documentary photographs and field chronicles to breathe naturally.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with 32px gutters and 64px container padding. Section containers balance broad visual banners with offset 2-to-3 column editorial layouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 24px gutters and 40px outer padding. Cards fluidly collapse into dual columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px margins. Dynamic hero text steps down to mobile-specific typography tokens while maintaining generous touch targets (minimum 48px height).
- **Rhythmic White Space:** Content blocks honor intentional white space as an expression of peace and clarity. Ample top/bottom section padding (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) divides thematic narratives (e.g., from Sky Blue devotionals to Earth Green outreach reports).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, ambient diffusion and subtle structural borders rather than stark drop shadows:

- **Ambient Warm Shadows:** Layered with a hint of warm earth umber (`rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.06)`) rather than synthetic gray, simulating natural East African sunlight. Elevations elevate from a resting level (`0 2px 8px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.04)`) to a hovered/modal state (`0 12px 32px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.08)`).
- **Framed Insets & Textile Borders:** Authentic photography is framed with subtle 1px border outlines tinted in warm stone (`#EADFD5`), evoking museum-grade gallery mats and hand-bound travel journals.
- **Tonal Layering:** The primary application relies on warm tinted surface tiers (Canvas `#FCFBF7` -> Card `#FFFFFF` -> Accent Surface `#F5EFEB`). Floating overlays employ high-clarity scrims with slight backdrop warm filtering.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced roundedness (`roundedness: 2`, where base components feature `0.5rem` / `8px` radii). This geometry conveys welcoming softness while keeping enough structure to complement geometric Shuka striping motifs:

- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Configured with `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius for standard buttons, and full pill rounding (`9999px`) reserved exclusively for category tags, status badges, and social proof chips.
- **Photography & Visual Media:** Framed with gentle `1rem` (`16px`) corners, preventing harsh cutoffs and emphasizing humanity in every captured face.
- **Hero & Content Cards:** Built with `1rem` or `1.5rem` outer bounds, pairing seamlessly with internal 1px warm borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Bravery Red):** Filled `#C92A2A` background, pure white label, `0.5rem` radius, and subtle warm shadow. Used strictly for primary conversion actions ("Support the Mission", "Donate Now"). Hover state shifts to `#A91E1E` with a slight upward translate (`-1px`).
- **Secondary (Sky Blue):** Deep `#1E429F` container with white text, utilized for field updates and missionary profile explorations.
- **Tertiary / Warm Outline:** 1.5px solid border in `#1A1715` with transparent background, designed for secondary actions like "Download Field Report" or "Read Story".
- **Hospitality Action (Ochre):** Filled `#E08722` button dedicated to volunteer recruitment and "Get In Touch" forms.

### Cards & Story Tiles
- **Story Cards:** Built with a crisp `#FFFFFF` background over the off-white canvas, framed by a delicate 1px border in `#EADFD5`. Features full-bleed or inset photography with a 16:9 or 4:5 aspect ratio, accompanied by an Ochre or Green topic chip and a Noto Serif headline.
- **Outreach & Impact Cards:** Highlighted with a top 4px accent border in Community Green (`#1E7E34`) or Bravery Red (`#C92A2A`), reinforcing narrative connection through color symbolism.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Focus Badges:** Full-pill silhouette with a soft pastel-tinted background and deep foreground text (e.g., Land Green background `#E8F5E9` with `#1E7E34` text for "Community Health").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Inputs:** Clean white backgrounds with 1px border `#D4C7BA`, transitioning to a 2px Shuka Red or Sky Blue focus ring with warm glow. Labels sit outside the inputs in `Work Sans` 600 weight for unequivocal legibility under high-contrast environments.

### Lists & Geometric Accents
- **Story Lists:** Separated by ultra-fine warm dividers (`#EDE4DA`). Bulleted lists within mission reports incorporate subtle diamond-shaped geometric accents derived from traditional beadwork geometry instead of standard circular dots.
