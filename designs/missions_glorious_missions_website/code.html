The Great Rift Pastoralist Water Infrastructure, Drought Resilience, and Holistic Community Health Initiative of Northern Samburu District

Deploying high-yield solar aquifer systems across deep arid plains to eradicate water-borne pathogens and alleviate the 14-kilometer daily trek traditionally borne by mothers and young girls.

Integrated alongside hygienic livestock distribution centers, mobile triage teams administer primary diagnostics, neonatal supplements, and sanitary training directly into remote pastoral clusters during seasonal migration cycles.
